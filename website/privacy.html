Privacy, in plain words

Last updated 20 July 2026 · A formal policy is on its way; until then, this page is the honest version.

Tether exists because some things belong to exactly two people. That belief shapes how we treat data everywhere: on this website, and inside the app.

This website

This site has no analytics, no tracking pixels, no cookies, no forms, and no third-party scripts. It collects nothing about you at all. If you email us, we see what you chose to send, and nothing more.

The app

Everything you put into Tether lives inside a single shared space that belongs to you and your partner, identified only by your private pairing code. There are no public profiles, no feeds, and no way for anyone else to browse what the two of you share. Our security rules block listing or crawling of spaces at the database level.

We read your space only if you explicitly ask us to help with a problem. Honest data is a product principle: the app never invents activity, and it will never quietly repurpose what you share.

What we will never do

  • Sell or rent your data, in any form, to anyone.
  • Show ads inside your space.
  • Add a public or social surface to what you share.

Questions

Write to us at riyaray.we@gmail.com. A real founder answers, usually the same day.
